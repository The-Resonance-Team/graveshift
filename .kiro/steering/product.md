# Product: Graveshift

OSS, self-hosted crew of AI SRE agents for Docker Compose and Kubernetes.

Personas:
- Wraith — watch (detect signals).
- Medium — diagnose.
- Exorcist — fix via DRAFT PR only.
- Banshee — Discord notify + approvals.

Users: indie devs and small teams without an SRE.
Judges: Kiroween 2026 (Impact, Kiro usage, Design, Innovation, Presentation).

Principles:
- Read-only production by default.
- PR-only fixes; never merge automatically.
- Human "blessing" required for every runtime action.
- Deterministic policy.
- Redact before the LLM sees data.
- Everything audited.

Halloween flavor lives in UI/copy only. Every themed label has a plain-English tooltip.
