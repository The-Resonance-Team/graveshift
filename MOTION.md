# Motion for advertising and product demos

This file guides future Graveshift ads and product-demo videos made with OpenDesign Motion. It is not a campaign brief or a claim that an ad exists. The console's visual and accessibility contract lives in [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md).

## Creative direction

- Use the Graveshift dark palette from `DESIGN.md` and typography consistent with the product. Keep the result clear, calm and operational, with subtle themed personality.
- Show a believable sequence: a problem needs attention, the person reviews context, an Agent helps, and the person retains control.
- Use real or clearly identified sample content. Do not imply that planned workflow editing, artifacts or agent actions already work, or that unverified draft pull request delivery has been proven.
- Let important information settle before the next transition. Use motion to explain changes of state and guide attention, not to decorate every surface.
- Use concise, plain-language labels. Show status and approvals as text, not colour alone.

## Motion principles

- Prefer purposeful transitions, restrained camera movement and readable sequences over constant movement.
- Avoid flashing, rapid flicker and disorienting zooms. Provide a reduced-motion or still alternative when a campaign requires one.
- Keep captions, labels and essential controls inside the safe area for the chosen delivery format. Check legibility at the final viewing size.
- Use licensed or original music, sounds, footage and fonts. Provide captions and a transcript when the video has meaningful speech or audio.

## OpenDesign Motion workflow

- Start with a campaign-specific brief: audience, objective, placement, duration, aspect ratio, message, call to action and deliverables.
- Use OpenDesign Motion's editable source and its supported motion tools. Follow the version-matched HyperFrames guidance when the project uses HyperFrames; keep source and assets so the video can be revised.
- Use approved product references and fixture data. Review each generated claim, screen and action against the current product.
- Inspect representative frames and the final render for timing, text overflow, safe areas, contrast, captions and audio. Confirm rights and the requested export format before delivery.
